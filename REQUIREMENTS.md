# Requirements

This is the Cadence HR team's own list of what the system must do. It is the scope of the challenge. Every numbered requirement needs an answer in your build. Refer to them by number, for example "4.3".

## 1. Keep candidate information in one place

The system should:

- **1.1** Keep all CVs and candidate details in one place.
- **1.2** Let the team move its current CVs, Excel lists and recruitment information into the system.
- **1.3** Create one profile for each candidate.
- **1.4** Show, for each candidate:
  - Name and contact details
  - Work experience
  - Education
  - Skills
  - Qualifications
  - Certificates
  - Previous applications
  - Years of experience
- **1.5** Keep the candidate's information and CV up to date.

## 2. Search and read CVs

The system should:

- **2.1** Read CVs in PDF and Word.
- **2.2** Search inside every CV, wherever it is stored, not only by file name.
- **2.3** Let the team search and filter candidates by:
  - Name
  - Skills
  - Years of experience
  - Education
  - Qualifications
  - Certificates
  - Industry experience
  - Current or previous job title
  - Nationality, when the role requires it
- **2.4** Show search results in a clear and simple way.

## 3. Find suitable candidates

The system should:

- **3.1** Compare CVs with the job description and the company's requirements.
- **3.2** Help the team find the candidates who best meet the requirements.
- **3.3** Clearly show:
  - The requirements the candidate meets
  - Their relevant skills and experience
  - Their main strengths
  - Any important experience or requirements they do not have
- **3.4** Give a short, clear summary of each candidate.
- **3.5** Let the Cadence HR team review the results before any candidate is shared with a company.

## 4. Find duplicate profiles

The HR team has held CVs and candidate information for many years. The system should:

- **4.1** Find candidates who have applied more than once and update their profile.
- **4.2** Check for repeated profiles using:
  - Name
  - Email address
  - Phone number
- **4.3** Update the existing profile rather than create another one for the same candidate.
- **4.4** Let the team merge repeated profiles.
- **4.5** Keep the candidate's latest application.

## 5. Track company recruitment requests

The system should track recruitment requests and keep their history. For each request it should show:

- **5.1** The company requesting support
- **5.2** The job title
- **5.3** The job description and requirements
- **5.4** The date the request was received
- **5.5** The Cadence employee handling the request
- **5.6** The candidates found for the role
- **5.7** The candidates shared with the company
- **5.8** An export of this information into a quarterly report

The system should also let the team:

- **5.9** Add notes and comments.
- **5.10** Upload related files.
- **5.11** Add updates and next steps.
- **5.12** Keep a clear record of the support provided.

## 6. Add candidates from other sources

The system should:

- **6.1** Let the team add candidates found on LinkedIn or through other approved sources.
- **6.2** Let the team add their contact details and CV.
- **6.3** Check whether the candidate already exists before creating a new profile.
- **6.4** Show where the candidate was found.

## 7. Keep information safe

The system should:

- **7.1** Keep candidate and recruitment information private.
- **7.2** Let Cadence download or move its information when needed.
- **7.3** Allow only approved employees to access the system.
- **7.4** Meet Cadence's information security and privacy requirements.
- **7.5** Keep a record of important actions taken in the system.
- **7.6** Keep backup copies of the information.
- **7.7** Define how long documents are kept.

## 8. Simple and easy to use

The system should:

- **8.1** Be easy for the HR team to understand and use.
- **8.2** Bring candidate profiles, company requests and reports into one place.
- **8.3** Work with Cadence's current systems where needed. Today those are SharePoint, OneDrive, Outlook and Excel.
- **8.4** Allow new features or changes to be added later.
- **8.5** Work on computers and mobile phones.

## Overall requirement

The HR team wants a simple, secure recruitment system that keeps candidate information in one place. It should help the team search CVs, find suitable candidates, remove repeated profiles and track the recruitment support it gives to companies.

The aim is to save time, cut manual work, keep information organised and help the team give companies faster, better recruitment support.
